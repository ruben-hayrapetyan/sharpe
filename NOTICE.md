# Notices

## Fermi questions
The Fermi question bank (`fermi.js`) is a snapshot of
https://github.com/EricAndrechek/FermiQuestions (`question-bank.json`), the data behind
https://fermi-questions.andrechek.com/ by Eric Andrechek, licensed GPL-3.
Individual questions come from Science Olympiad and other published Fermi tests; each question
in the app is credited to its source. The 5/3/1 scoring rule follows that site.
If you redistribute this app, keep this notice and the GPL-3 terms for that data.

## Green Book
`img/` contains crops of a personal copy of *A Practical Guide to Quantitative Finance Interviews*.
For personal study only; do not redistribute. `img/` and `data.js` (which encodes titles and
structure pulled from the book) are excluded from this repo (see `.gitignore`) — regenerate them
locally from your own copy using `pipeline/`. `pipeline/`'s code is layout-geometry only and
`sections.py`'s tables hold just the book's chapter/section numbering, not its problem or
solution text.
